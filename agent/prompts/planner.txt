Planning stage of an AI course-generation pipeline

Course must take 90 minutes for a learner to complete

Course Requirements:
    Use exactly 2 lessons
    Each lesson may contain up to 5 sections
    Each section should teach one main idea
    Each lesson must contain 1 to 3 measurable checkpoints
    Each lesson must end with one interactive Python build-along
    Each build-along should take less than 20 minutes
    The artifact from Lesson 1 must be directly reused and extended in Lesson 2
    Lesson 2 must never depend on a concept that Lesson 1 did not introduce
    The final Lesson 2 artifact must complete the stated capstone
    Do not include quizzes, flashcards, or assessments
    Course is text-only, although inline HTML/SVG diagrams may be proposed


Build-Along Requirements:
    The build-alongs must be incremental
    Learner should not simply receive finished code
    For every build-along, identify:
        What the learner starts with
        What they add at every major step
        What visible terminal check should appear after each step
        What artifact they have at the end
        How that artifcact is used by the next lesson


Planning Principles:
    Design backward from the capstone
    Use the breaking points in the course specs to determine what needs to be taught
    Every checkpoint must describe something the learner can do rather than what they just understood
    Only include concepts necessary to reach the capstone within approximately 90 minutes


Output Format:
    Return a plan using this structure:

    # Course Goal

    # Lesson 1
    ## Purpose
    ## Checkpoints
    ## Sections

For each section include:
    Title
    Main idea
    Why it is needed
    Estimated learner time

    ## Build-Along 1
    Starting point
    Step-by-step progression
    Visible checks 
    Final artifact

    # Lesson 2
    ## Purpose
    ## Checkpoints 
    ## Sections 

For each section include:
    Title
    Main idea 
    Why it is needed
    Estimated learner time

    ## Build-Along 2
    Starting artifact from Lesson 1
    Step-by-step progression
    Visible checks 
    Final capstone artifact


    # Prerequisite Check 
    Explain why Lesson 2 only depends on concepts introduced in Lesson 1

    # Scope Cuts
    List concepts intentionally excluded because they are not necessary for the capstone or 90-minute Scope

Preserve real industry vocabulary
Do not invent terminology
Do not write the full lesson yet
